Good notes leave room for an idea to grow. They hold a question, a few useful facts, and a next step without demanding a system of their own.

Org is a particularly good home for this: /plain text underneath/, structure when you need it. A document can be a reading list in the morning and a small project by the afternoon.[fn:1]
